## About Amsterdam

A stop in Amsterdam offers the chance to explore the sights of one of Europe’s most colorful, dynamic and historic cities—one with a well-earned reputation as a laid-back and inviting place for people of all stripes. Visitors are naturally drawn to the historic city center where you’ll find some of the world’s top art museums, including the Rijksmuseum and the Van Gogh Museum. And at Dam Square, the Amsterdam’s largest public square, you can tour the Royal Palace before continuing to the tourist attractions on the Canal Belt. The iconic network of waterways that surrounds the downtown area offers a picturesque backdrop for sightseeing by bike or canal boat. Be sure to visit the floating Bloemenmarkt to peruse famed Dutch tulips, and take time to wander and window-shop among the narrow lanes of de Jordaan. And you won’t have to look far in Amsterdam to find delicious Dutch treats along the way. Just duck into a cozy brown café to sample a plate of bitterballen with mustard and a beer, and grab a gooey sweet stroopwafel from a street vendor as you stroll.

Drive through the beautiful Dutch landscape to **Delft** -- an age-old city best known for its famous blue pottery.

See the Weighing Houses, historical buildings and canals that typify many Dutch cities.

Stop at **Porcelyne Fles pottery** to see a copy of Rembrandt's Night Watch made in Delft Blue tiles. See the process of pottery-making and the results in the workshop and exposition room.

In Delft's city center, take a short, guided stroll along the streets where renowned Dutch artist Johannes Vermeer lived and created his greatest paintings. You'll have free time for lunch and to explore the Old Town at your leisure, take some photos and browse among the shops and boutiques.

Continue to **The Hague** -- the administrative center of the Netherlands and the city where the king and queen reside.

A **panoramic city** **tour** will take you past the old and new Parliament buildings as well as the Royal Palace and the Peace Palace.

Amazing **Amsterdam** has always had strong ties to the water. Spread out over 70 islands, this cosmopolitan city and capital of the Netherlands boasts 60 miles of canals and 1,000 bridges. As early as the 13th century, tiny ships sailed from this port into the challenging Zuider Sea.

Amsterdam was the birthplace of the Hanseatic League and gained renown in the 15th century when it became a major trading center, steadily assuming commercial leadership and thus controlling the country's finances. This trend remains to this day. Both future-oriented and steeped in history, Amsterdam is most famous for its narrow, gabled houses lining the canals.

After a short transfer by coach to the city center, you will begin a brief **panoramic city tour**. Watch for the major historic landmarks and buildings, including the Jewish Quarter, a windmill, the Tropenmuseum and Java Island.

Boarding a **glass-topped canal boat**, enjoy a one-hour cruise through **Amsterdam's canals**.

Finally, your coach will take you to **Schiphol Airport** in time for your flight.

**Notes:** There may be up to five steps to board the canal boat. Wear comfortable walking shoes. Bring sunscreen. This tour is available only to guests whose cruise ends in Amsterdam, with flights departing after 3pm. If you have already purchased an airport transfer with your cruise booking, a refund for the transfer will be processed on board the ship, as this excursion automatically includes a transfer. Tour does not operate on holidays.
